# FLOWS — product film

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Flows turns a creative idea into a connected, editable AI workflow on one canvas.
- **Identity:** A nocturnal studio palette drawn from the supplied Flows screenshot: graphite ink, soft chalk, electric periwinkle and a small ember-orange signal. Space Grotesk display, DM Sans support, JetBrains Mono for coordinates.
- **Hero:** The canvas itself: image, prompt, director and video nodes become a visual system rather than a stack of app screenshots.
- **Motion:** Topological cable-draws; a virtual camera that punches into and pulls out of connected nodes; compound scale-through / split-panel transitions carrying a connector bead.
- **Arc:** A loose idea lands, connections make it legible, Director AI expands it, generation resolves into a result, then the whole system folds into a single Flows mark.
- **Payoff:** The final canvas contracts to a bright square mark, then that mark expands back into the first node's origin.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 24 seconds.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.80s / 4.80s — “START WITH A THOUGHT.”

**Purpose:** Establish that a workflow starts as an idea, not a complicated setup.

**Composition and artistic idea:** On a charcoal canvas, a huge cropped field of fine grid points sits behind a floating Prompt node in the upper-left quadrant. A smaller Image Upload node occupies lower-left; an empty ModelArk Video node waits on the right. The prompt node is 54% of frame width; the right node is 40%. Chalk text; lavender active edge; only one orange point indicates the next connection. The idea's three words occupy the prompt like ink, not as a separate title card.

**Exact content:** Eyebrow “A CREATIVE WORKFLOW, IN ONE PLACE” at 0.00s, upper left, 1.8vmin, remains until 1.2s. Prompt text begins as “Cinematic” with the node; “pan over the scene” resolves at 0.70s. Main line “Start with a thought.” reveals at 2.1s in the lower-right zone, 5.3vmin, and holds until handoff. Tiny top-left brand label “F / FLOWS” remains throughout.

**Choreography:** 0.00s: eyebrow/rule is on screen; prompt node snaps into view at 0.08s already 20% visible within the frame. At 0.15s the first grid plane slides 1.5% diagonally. At 0.70s the image-reference node snaps from depth and the final prompt words resolve; at 1.20s the video node snaps in. Each snap has a stiff spring (>=240), with no gradual loading scale. 2.1s: title mask opens downward as the camera travels toward the first node's output port. 3.45s: one lilac connector bead lights, and a thin dashed route animates out from the prompt. 4.1–4.8s: camera pushes through that bead; bead remains centered while card edges split into four dark panels.

**Transition contract:** Connector bead at 76%, 39% of frame grows to a full-frame circular aperture; the four card-edge panels peel behind it. Incoming scene starts on the same bead, with its cable immediately drawing from that exact point.

**Assets and technique:** Screenshot is a visual reference only; build the UI as vector/CSS artwork with accurate node labels and connection ports. Fonts are loaded locally by the scaffold. CSS grid and masked typography; no external image assets.

**Sound intent:** Original instrumental, soft percussive click on each of three snaps; no generated SFX. Cut is aimed at a musical accent, alignment unverified until the track is measured.

### Shot 02 / 4.80–9.60s / 4.80s — “MAKE THE CONNECTION.”

**Purpose:** Show the core canvas metaphor: multiple inputs connect into one generation.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Open macro on a single curved cable, oversized like an illuminated violet road, with an orange connector bead carried from the previous shot. Camera pulls back to disclose a wide three-node topology: Prompt and Image Upload flow into ModelArk Video. Card faces remain dark, with chalk labels and electric-violet path.

**Exact content:** Hero word “CONNECT” appears 5.25–7.1s as a 9vmin line behind the cable. At 6.65s, a small 3.1vmin caption “Prompts + references, together.” appears bottom left and remains through 8.75s. Node labels “Prompt”, “Image Upload”, “ModelArk Video” are always visible on their card faces. Footer detail “TEXT TO VIDEO” appears in the video node from 7.2s.

**Choreography:** 0.0–0.42s: bead grows 1.0→2.1 and passes through lens, cable draws with it. 0.40–1.30s: cable bends in a Bezier arc from left edge to right; camera rolls 1.5° and pulls back. 1.25s: macro crop opens into node topology via four-panel split, while “CONNECT” unmasks behind cable. 2.0s: prompt and image nodes become visible in contrasting vertical bands. 2.6s: cable snaps into two destination ports; bead makes a small responsive bounce at each split. 3.15s: caption resolves; 3.7s: right video preview shutter animates open. 4.0–4.8s: the preview rectangle folds into a three-blade iris; camera punches into its center as a Director icon begins its reveal.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing preview iris is the same rounded-rectangle frame as the incoming director node's image-like output marker; cable bead continues through its center. A 3D fold joins the iris and diagonal split of the next shot.

**Assets and technique:** SVG paths for deterministic connector routing; CSS cable highlight and repeated grid; node cards recreated from supplied reference.

**Sound intent:** Music bed; aligned cut to the nearest measured onset. No VO/SFX.

### Shot 03 / 9.60–14.40s / 4.80s — “LET THE DIRECTOR OPEN IT UP.”

**Purpose:** Reveal the Director AI as a way to turn one brief into an organized sequence of scene prompts.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Director node anchors just left of center, 35% of frame width; a strip of three scene cards grows vertically and then travels horizontally across the frame, like one prompt unfolding into shots. Orange director marker; matte black-blue canvas; cards each have a different framing diagram to avoid a generic repeated stack.

**Exact content:** Eyebrow “DIRECTOR AI” at 9.72s in upper-left safe region. Main phrase “One brief. / A whole sequence.” at 4.5vmin appears 10.2s, held until 13.5s. Script inside node reads “A cinematic pan over the scene.” Scene labels at 10.9, 11.6, 12.3s: “01 / Establish”, “02 / Move”, “03 / Resolve”. At 13.35s bottom-right support line “Scene prompts, built from your idea.” appears until exit.

**Choreography:** Start with incoming iris resolving at center and camera already 1.45x / 10px soft. 0.0–0.78s: director icon sharpens and camera settles to 1.0; panel seam travels left-to-right to expose the node. 0.72s: first scene card unfolds on a hinge from the director's right edge; 1.35 and 1.95s: cards 2 and 3 follow, each deeper in perspective before snapping front-on. 2.4s: world camera whip-pan 0.48s to card 1, then holds long enough to read its thumbnail geometry; 3.05s: camera tracks across card 2 to card 3, while image framing lines redraw in each card. 3.75s: scene strip rotates toward lens; three cards shear into a single overlapping frame. 4.0–4.8s: 3.5x push-through dissolves the scaled card plane with opacity [1,1,0], blur 0→14px; incoming generation panel starts before exit completes.

**Transition contract:** Card 3's bright edge scales through the lens and dissolves; the next shot's generation preview opens on the same perspective and its image corner settles to the matching position.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG composition thumbnails (wide pan, close-up, end-frame). Director icon and node typography are graphic artwork.

**Sound intent:** Music bed, cuts aimed to measured accents. No VO/SFX.

### Shot 04 / 14.40–19.20s / 4.80s — “FROM PROMPT TO GENERATION.”

**Purpose:** Make image/video generation feel like a connected continuation, not a separate tool.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Begin on a split plane: a reference-image card occupies left 44%; a large ModelArk video node occupies right 56%. A bright prompt strip threads between them. On the reveal the node's checkerboard preview is replaced by a bespoke abstract generated landscape: a dark cobalt horizon and a single sculptural lavender moon made with SVG/CSS. This is a graphic product preview, not a claim of a real generation result.

**Exact content:** Headline “MAKE IT / MOVE.” appears at 14.72s in the left top quadrant, 5vmin. Tiny model label “SEEDANCE · VIDEO” appears in the model node at 15.1s. At 16.25s caption “Image and prompt in. Motion out.” enters bottom-left and remains through 18.4s. Bottom-right pill-shaped status (purely visual, not a CTA) changes “PREPARING” → “READY” at 17.65s.

**Choreography:** 0.0–0.38s: incoming frame settles while split edge moves from 53% to 45%. 0.4–1.0s: prompt ribbon travels from reference card to video node; heading's second line reveals on its wake. 1.2s: preview aperture opens radially from the model node's port; violet moon appears at 0.3 scale, then comes forward to occupy ~66% of short-edge height. 2.0s: its surface gains a thin moving contour line; its shadow is a separate fading layer. 2.6s: the horizon pans at 12% parallax, while two image registration corners lock into place. 3.2s: status flips to “READY”, caption settles. 3.85–4.8s: the result zooms back into a 16:9 thumbnail as surrounding nodes re-enter at the edge; the camera pulls out to a connected canvas overview.

**Transition contract:** Moon's circular silhouette collapses into the rounded preview tile; tile is one of three objects brought into the final canvas. Its last position matches the tile's initial position in the final.

**Assets and technique:** Build reference and result as bespoke vector/HTML UI artwork; no generated or stock photos imply app output. SVG aperture and contour; layered CSS fields.

**Sound intent:** Music bed only; align aperture opening with nearest useful track accent after RMS scan.

### Shot 05 / 19.20–24.00s / 4.80s — “ONE CANVAS. MANY DIRECTIONS.”

**Purpose:** Close on the differentiator: a connected, flexible place to make and extend a creative workflow.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Palette reverses from deep ink to warm paper. The full canvas diagram, first glimpsed as separate tools, compresses with elegant topology into one violet square; the square becomes the Flows mark, not a copied logo. Wordmark is plain typed “Flows” because no brand logo asset was supplied. Centered end-lockup with ample paper field, thin graph lines quietly framing it.

**Exact content:** Word burst “IDEA.” at 19.20s, “WORKFLOW.” at 19.72s, “POSSIBILITY.” at 20.18s, each 30vmin with overlapping scale/blur replacement. Main mark resolves 21.02s; plain wordmark “Flows” resolves 21.02s. Closing line “A canvas for what comes next.” appears 21.52s at 3.1vmin. Lockup readable to final frame.

**Choreography:** 0.0–0.35s: wide overview pulls into warm paper field while tile topology remains in motion. 0.00, 0.52, 0.98s: the three words punch on the measured 100 BPM grid; node outlines translate into their letter silhouettes before each word locks. 1.82s: connector network tightens into a luminous periwinkle square and the card edges unfold outward. 1.82–2.15s: mark snaps into center at 1.08 scale then settles; wordmark tracks in letter by letter (45ms offsets). 2.32s: endline unmasks. 3.0s: quiet constellation line arcs around the lockup. 3.86–4.71s: mark contracts to a 12px square at the exact upper-left origin of Shot 01's prompt node as that prompt-node frame appears at 20% opacity; next loop expands it into the node. No blank or static-only tail.

**Transition contract:** Centered square, pale core first. It shrinks while traveling to the opening prompt-node anchor; first shot begins with that same small square already visible and grows on its first stiff snap. Palette changes by match geometry, not fade-to-black.

**Assets and technique:** Type-only wordmark; handmade node topology; field inversion, morph via clipping and transform. No invented logo asset, release claim, or CTA.

**Sound intent:** Music resolves on the final square; no extra SFX or voiceover.

## Variety and feasibility review

Five main shots with alternating macro/wide, asymmetrical/centered, dark/light, node/typographic graphic systems. Each has a named mechanism and distinct carrier; none repeats the same headline-plus-product layout. Action continues after every entrance, the first beat is immediate, content changes more often than every second, and each shot's final 15–20% is an outgoing transformation. The image/video result is intentionally vector art rather than a simulated factual generation; no physical product photography is needed for this software canvas.
